# 星灯りの村: story design (approved 2026-10-02)

## Premise
The Wishing Crystal (願いのクリスタル) at the heart of Clover Village is fading. Its light is what holds the village
fence: **the brighter the crystal, the larger the village.** Nine Titans have swallowed the starlight of the nine
worlds, and three friends who went looking for the cause were caged by bosses. The explorer rescues them, defeats the
Titans to win the light back and conquers each world's stars. Every return of light brightens the crystal and the
village grows (village rank).

## Characters
| Character | Role |
|---|---|
| ルミ (Lumi), the crystal spirit | Story giver; speaks in a dialogue panel. No new 3D model: light and a portrait |
| スプラウト (Sprout) | First friend, caged by the Ancient Treant; tends the garden once home |
| ペッパー (Pepper) | Second friend, caged by the Giant Toy Robot on Toybox; cooks |
| クローバー (Clover) | Third friend, caged by King Bear; looks after the animals |
| Nine Titans | "Starlight eaters", one per world (existing models and skills) |

## Five arcs, twenty chapters, five village ranks
| Arc | Chapters | Levels | Story | Arc reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 一. 小さな灯り | 1–4 | 1–15 | Lumi wakes; garden, market, first fights; rescue Sprout | Village rank 2: orchard |
| 二. 星の海へ | 5–8 | 15–45 | Starship to Toybox, rescue Pepper; pen and forge; climax: King Bear, rescue Clover | Rank 3: big pasture, second pond |
| 三. 九つの影 | 9–12 | 45–65 | The Titans revealed; first five Titans; ★3 on worlds | Rank 4: decoration plaza, second room |
| 四. 星を取り戻せ | 13–16 | 65–85 | Last four Titans; Titans ★5; forge +10; collections | Rank 5: friends' houses, star deck |
| 五. 星灯りの守り人 | 17–20 | 85–100 | Titans ★8, worlds ★8, forge +15; finale: the crystal fully lit, a star festival | Title 「星灯りの守り人」, legendary outfit |

Each chapter: Lumi's opening lines, 5–7 goals built on existing systems, a closing event, and a visible unlock
(friend, village rank, outfit, pet, recipe, title, keepsake decoration).

## Side lines
- Friend chains (5 steps each, after the rescue): Sprout's orchard, Pepper's star dish, Clover's four animals.
- Planet tales (3 steps per world, after discovery).
- Level milestones every 5 levels (gift box; titles at 20, 40, 60, 80, 100).
- Collection rewards (trophy decoration and title per completed set and the fish log).
Hourly, daily, weekly tasks, village orders and bounties stay as the everyday layer.

## Delivery
1. Story system: Lumi dialogue, twenty chapters, unlock rewards, titles, village rank stored.
2. Village rank in the world: fence radius and new zones per rank.
3. Side chains, level milestones, collection rewards.
